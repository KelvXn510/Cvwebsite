02 / About

Who I Am

I'm Abid, a student, tutor, and builder based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My teaching philosophy is simple: real understanding beats memorisation every time. When a student genuinely grasps a concept, they don't just pass an exam. They build the confidence to tackle harder problems on their own, without needing to be hand-held through every step.

I started at Rajuk Uttara Model College, then moved to Rajshahi Cadet College for classes 7 and 8, where I learned what discipline and structure actually feel like. After that I completed my SSC at Bangladesh International School and College, and I'm currently studying A/S Level subjects privately, with Physics, Mathematics, Further Mathematics, and Computer Science as my focus areas.

Outside of studying I'm building a high-power rocket and a go-kart from scratch. Not as hobbies really, but because I genuinely want to see the physics work in front of me.

03 / Tutoring

How I Teach

Every session is built around your child's pace, learning style, and curriculum needs.

Concept-First Approach

Rather than drilling formulas, I make sure students understand the "why" behind every topic. This creates lasting retention and genuine academic confidence.

Tailored Practice

Each student's revision material is structured around their specific weaknesses. No one-size-fits-all worksheets, just targeted problem sets that build on what they already know.

Patient Communication

No question is too small. I try to make sessions feel like a space where students can ask anything without worrying about looking silly, because that's honestly where most real learning starts.

Progress Tracking

I keep parents in the loop with honest, practical updates on their child's progress: what's improved, what still needs work, and what the plan looks like going forward.
